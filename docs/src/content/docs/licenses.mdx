### Embedding models

clanka does not ship an embedding model; [`clanka embedding`](/cli/embedding/)
downloads one on first use. Both are licensed under Apache-2.0:

- `english`: [Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2](https://huggingface.co/Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2),
  an ONNX conversion of
  [sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2).
- `multilingual`:
  [Qdrant/paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2-onnx-Q](https://huggingface.co/Qdrant/paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2-onnx-Q),
  a quantized ONNX conversion of
  [sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2).
