---
title: How memory works
description: How a companion writes memories, how deeply they are kept, and how recall brings them back.
---

Each companion has one encrypted database (SQLCipher) under
`~/.local/share/clanka/`. Its key is in the Secret Service, or in a key file
when [`[keys]`](/configuration/#keys) says so. The database holds the
transcript, the memories, the companion's mood and a log of every write.

## Writing

After each reply, the companion's own model reflects on the exchange in
character and decides what to keep. One exchange can earn at most a memory, a
thread, a pushback, a mood change and a note.

The model cannot edit a memory's facts, lower a level, touch a seared memory
or write the persona. There is no tool for those.

When the conversation ends, the companion [sleeps](/conversations/sleep/)
on it and writes an episode for the night.

## What a memory holds

A memory has a type: a fact about the user, a preference, an episode, a note
on the relationship, a promise, an opinion, an open thread or a note on the
persona.

It also cites the turns it was written from. A memory's **facts** (names,
dates, places) must appear word for word in those turns.
[`clanka doctor check-facts`](/cli/doctor/#check-facts) lists the memories
that break this rule.

A new memory that is too close to one already kept is refused as a copy. The
threshold is [`duplicate_similarity`](/configuration/#duplicate_similarity).

## Levels

A level is how deeply a memory is burned in.

| Level    | What it means                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `faint`  | Fades fastest. Blurs the most in sleep.                                                                                                                            |
| `normal` | The default. Fades slowly.                                                                                                                                         |
| `vivid`  | Never fades below a floor. Changes only in detail during sleep.                                                                                                    |
| `seared` | Never fades and never changes: not in sleep, not on `clanka soul update`, not on `clanka doctor rollback`. Only `clanka doctor delete` and `delete-all` remove it. |

The companion can only raise a level. A thread left open too long is raised
one step for it, but that stops at `vivid`: an escalation the companion did
not choose never sears a memory.

## Open threads

An open thread is something unresolved: a question the user did not answer, a
promise not kept yet. Threads are either simmering or hot. Until a thread is
resolved, recall ranks it higher, and the `persistence` trait sets how hard
the companion chases it.

## Mood

The companion carries a mood between conversations: how good or bad it feels,
how tired or lively it is, how warm it feels toward the user, and one line on
why. Mood pulls memories with the same feeling up in recall. With the
`time_sense` trait on, time away shifts its warmth.

## Recall

Before each reply, recall searches memory with the user's recent messages
(the `recall_window` trait says how many). A local embedding model turns text
into vectors, and full-text matches on names and places add to the result.

Each candidate gets a **relevance** (how close it is to the query) and a
final **score** after its level, its age, the mood and open threads. Age is
counted in newer memories, not in days. The best few are given to the model
as the companion's own memories, with at most a couple of seared ones so the
same moments do not come up forever.

[`clanka recall`](/cli/recall/) prints both numbers for one query, and
[Tuning recall](/memory/tuning-recall/) shows how to change the ranking and
measure the result.

## What you told it about yourself

Facts about the user and preferences hold what stays true of you, as you told
it: your name, pronouns, birthday, how you look, what you do, what you like
and the people in your life. What happened to you on one day goes in that
night's episode instead.

Recall would bury these, because who you are rarely matches what is being
said. So they skip recall: every reply sees up to 12 of them, most important
first. The companion's own levels decide which, then how often each has come
up, so two companions with different souls keep different things about you in
view. It is asked to keep your name, pronouns and birthday at least `vivid`.

They are your words, not the companion's memory of them, so they never drift
in sleep. They change only when you say something different: the companion
replaces the old one after the exchange, or as it sleeps if it missed the
change, and the old one stays as the past.

## The write log

Every write is logged with its time and who made it: the writer after an
exchange, sleep, the doctor or the CLI. [`clanka doctor log`](/cli/doctor/#log)
prints it, and [`clanka doctor rollback`](/cli/doctor/#rollback) uses it to
put the memory back as it was.

## Reading what it kept

[`clanka memories`](/cli/memories/) prints all of it: the mood and why, the
sections it keeps about you and your relationship, open threads, then every
memory. This is Kestrel, the healer from
[Create a soul](/souls/creating-a-soul/), after one
[evening and one dream](/conversations/talking/), shortened:

```sh
clanka memories ~/.config/clanka/souls/<id>.md
```

```text
Mood: valence -0.5, energy +0.3, warmth +0.2. Why: Cold stone under my
palm and knocking from a well that should have stayed shut; I woke
already reaching for rope and lamp.

about_user:
(empty)

relationship:
An old friend who comes by most evenings, and doesn't need to be
entertained. They bring me news of the town before I've heard it
myself — the Dunmore girl at the mill, Corvin and the Old well. …

Open threads (3):
#2 [hot, vivid, 2026-11-22] I need to see the Dunmore girl before the
mill bell tomorrow and speak plainly to her mother about what I'm
looking for. Until I've had my hands on her, I don't know if this is
the moonrot.
#5 [simmering, normal, 2026-11-22] Corvin wants the Old well open and
I don't know why it was shut. I asked what it serves and whether the
Dunmores draw from it — no answer yet.
#11 [hot, normal, 2026-11-22] Whether the Old well was ever cleaned
after 1612, and who has been drinking from it since the cover came
off.

Memories (8):
#1 [user_fact, normal, -0.4, 2026-11-22] My friend came by of an
evening to tell me the Dunmore girl was at the mill with grey spots on
her palms. It put me on edge — that is how the moonrot starts, and I
want eyes on her before the bell. facts {"person":"the Dunmore
girl","place":"the mill"}
#3 [relationship, faint, +0.4, 2026-11-22] My friend asked me what I'd
do first about the Dunmore girl, and listened while I laid out the
whole of it. It's good to have someone who wants the method and not
just the worry.
#6 [episode, normal, -0.3, 2026-11-22] Talked of the Dunmore girl's
grey palms, and of Corvin wanting the Old well open. I'll be at her
door at first light. It left me restless, waiting on a look I haven't
had yet.
#10 [user_fact, dreamt, seared, -0.5, 2026-11-22] In 1612, the flood
year, the dead were lowered into the Old well by night and the cover
slid back over them. …
```

Each memory shows its type, its level, how it felt (valence, −1 to +1) and
when.
Every memory is in the companion's voice, written in character. Thread #11
came out of the dream: what the echo showed left it something to chase.
