---
title: How lore is read
description: Where lore lives, which companion reads which entry, and when an entry opens.
---

Lore is reference for a story world: how magic works, the layout of a house
or a dungeon, a disease thought incurable. You write it once and every
companion you grant it to reads it. What a companion does in the story, and
what it learns, it adds beside your text, so the next companion that reads
the entry knows it too. One world, one timeline.

## Entries

An entry is a Markdown file in the lore directory: `lore/` beside `souls/`
(`~/.config/clanka/lore/` on Linux), or [`[lore] dir`](/configuration/#lore).
Its ID is its path without `.md`, in segments of a-z, 0-9 and `-`:
`moonrot`, `ashford/manor`.

```markdown
+++
title = "The moonrot"
aliases = ["rot", "the wasting"]
opens = ["ashford/wells"]
sealed = false
+++

The moonrot is a wasting sickness of the river towns. It is thought incurable.
```

| Key       | Default | Effect                                                                                       |
| --------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `title`   |         | Required. Naming it in a message opens the entry.                                            |
| `aliases` | `[]`    | Other names that open it. `/lore` and `read_lore` take a title over another entry's alias.   |
| `opens`   | `[]`    | Entries a companion that read this one may read from then on, without a grant.               |
| `sealed`  | `false` | Nothing a companion learns here is shared: no deeds, no released notes, only private notes. |

The text is yours. clanka never rewrites it; [`clanka lore`](/cli/lore/)
writes it only when you ask, and only after the frontmatter parses.

## Who reads what

A companion reads the entries its `soul.md` grants, in the frontmatter. A
trailing `/` grants a whole directory:

```toml
lore = ["moonrot", "ashford/"]
```

A companion from a cyberpunk world with `lore = ["night-city/"]` never sees
your entry on 1960s politics. A grant takes effect on the companion's next
turn; nothing has to restart. [`clanka soul lint`](/cli/soul/#lint) refuses
a malformed grant and warns about one that names no entry on this machine.

A companion won't talk while the lore it reads has an error
[`clanka lore lint`](/cli/lore/#lint) reports, such as an entry that doesn't
parse or a name two of its entries share. Lore it isn't granted doesn't
count.

## Reading

Lore has its own allotment, [`[lore] tokens`](/configuration/#lore) (3,000
estimated by default). It is apart from the memory budget and the
conversation's allowance, so lore never tires a companion.

On the first turn of a conversation the companion sees an index of the
entries it may read. An entry opens when:

- your message names its title or an alias (a name inside another entry's
  longer name doesn't count: "the Obsidian Guard" doesn't open an entry
  called "guard"),
- the companion's model calls `read_lore` (MCP and Hermes), or
- you send `/lore <id>` in `clanka chat` or Hermes.

It stays open while the allotment has room for it. Entries read most
recently come first, and of those one message names, the first named; one
that no longer fits closes until it is named again. An entry whose text alone
doesn't fit the allotment doesn't open, and the companion is told.

An open entry shows your text as how the world is, then the lines companions
added, strongest first, then the companion's own private notes. The index
marks an entry whose text, or whose lines from others, changed since the
companion last read it.
