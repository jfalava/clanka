---
title: Dreams and echoes
description: Send the companion into a dream, show it echoes of the past, and what stays when it wakes.
---

A message that starts with `~~` sends the companion into a dream. Sent in the
middle of a conversation, it ends the conversation first, as a goodnight
would, and the night is slept on behind the dream. What you write there is what happens to it, as "you". It lives the
dream in first person without knowing it is dreaming, with its temperament
louder and its life hazy.

In a dream, a message that starts with `<<` shows it an echo, a scene from
the past. Neither marker needs a space after it.

```text
you> ~~ You're standing in your mother's kitchen. It is too quiet.
you> << >>3 Years ago, in this room, the judge reads the sentence aloud.
```

## Waking

The companion wakes:

- when it chooses to, over something it cannot bear or that turns against a
  core value or a seared memory. It has to quote what you wrote in the dream:
  nothing it imagines wakes it.
- when the dream fills the session,
- on `/sleep` or `zz`: the reply is its last moment in the dream.

## What stays

Waking keeps the companion's mood, a few fragments that come back only in
later dreams, and at most one seared memory: a name, date or place that an
echo showed and that you had also written while it was awake. Anything the
dream made up fades.

Leaving mid-dream keeps nothing.

## Example

A real dream with Kestrel, the healer from
[Create a soul](/souls/creating-a-soul/). Earlier that evening, awake, the
user had mentioned the Old well and, out of earshot, the dead of 1612.

Once the wake pass is done, `clanka chat` says what stayed:

```text
Kestrel wakes: 3 fragments linger, 0 memories shifted; one thing an echo showed stays, burned in.
```

The fragments are faint images, marked `dreamt`, that come back only in later
dreams. The seared memory's facts are only what the echo showed and the user
had also said awake: the Old well and 1612.

```text
#7 [episode, dreamt, faint, -0.3] A stone rim, cold as a spring
morning, and my palm flat on it.
#8 [episode, dreamt, faint, -0.6] The knocking stopping all at once,
and the quiet after being worse.
#9 [episode, dreamt, faint, +0.1] A lamp held steady over black water,
and rope in my hands.
#10 [user_fact, dreamt, seared, -0.5] In 1612, the flood year, the
dead were lowered into the Old well by night and the cover slid back
over them. That is why it was covered — not for a season, but for
good. Anyone drawing from it now is drawing off the flood dead. facts
{"place":"Old well","year":"1612"}
```
