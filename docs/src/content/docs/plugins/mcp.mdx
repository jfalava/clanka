[`clanka mcp`](/cli/mcp/) serves one companion to an MCP host over stdio. It
is meant for trying a companion out in a host you already use. The host does
the talking and calls the tools.

## Add it to a host

Register `clanka mcp` as a stdio server, with the companion's `soul.md` as
its argument. Most hosts take a command and a list of arguments:

```json
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "nova": {
      "command": "clanka",
      "args": ["mcp", "/home/you/.config/clanka/souls/<id>.md"]
    }
  }
}
```

Use an absolute path: hosts start the command without a shell, so `~` is not expanded.

The server also offers the persona as an MCP prompt. It tells the host's
model who it is and how to use the tools.

## Tools

The host's model follows one fixed order on every user message:

| Tool         | When                         | What it does                                                                                                |
| ------------ | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `begin_turn` | First, before the reply      | Records the message and returns the companion's memories, mood and notes for this turn.                     |
| `end_turn`   | Last, after the reply        | Saves the reply so the exchange can be remembered. After a `zz` goodnight, it also sleeps.                  |
| `recall`     | Optional                     | Searches long-term memory on purpose. `begin_turn` already brings up what is relevant.                      |
| `read_lore`  | Optional                     | Opens a [lore](/lore/reading/) entry that `begin_turn` lists. An entry the user names opens by itself.       |
| `sleep`      | When the user says goodnight | Ends the conversation at once; it becomes memories in the background.                                       |
| `wake_up`    | In a dream                   | Asks to wake from a [dream](/conversations/dreams/), quoting the words that wake the companion. |

The doctor is never a tool. The [doctor token](/souls/traits/#the-doctor-token)
freezes memory, and the CLI does the rest.

## A turn

Kestrel, after one evening, as an MCP host's model sees her. Asked _"Is the
Old well safe to drink from?"_, the host calls `begin_turn` with the message
and gets back (wrapped here to fit):

```text
## Lore
How the world is: the text of each entry is canon. Lines under it are
what companions added: seen and told are as good as canon, claimed is
their word only, worked out and dreamt may be wrong.

Other entries you know of; one opens when it is named:
- ashford/corvin: Corvin Dunmore
- ashford/the-mill: The Mill
- ashford/wells: The Ashford wells
- moonrot: The moonrot

## Your relationship
Easy, and old enough that neither of us dresses it up. They come in
the evening, we talk over whatever's happened, and they go. No
ceremony about it. They carry news to me and I tell them straight what
I make of it, and they don't flinch at that. When I say I'll be
somewhere at first light, they know I mean it.

What the person you're talking to has told you about themselves:
- They came by in the evening to tell me the Dunmore girl has grey
  spots on her palms and that her mother won't let anyone look at her.
  Grey on the palms worries me; I want eyes on that girl. [girl:
  Dunmore, place: the mill]
- Corvin Dunmore means to open the Old well again rather than walk the
  hour a day to the Market well. I want the reason it was capped
  before he lifts the boards. [other well: the Market well, person:
  Corvin, well: the Old well]
- They still have my good knife from the honey harvest and keep
  forgetting to bring it back. I told them to keep it till harvest —
  I'd rather have it back before I go poking at that well. [event: the
  honey harvest]

Your mood: valence -0.3, energy +0.2, warmth toward the user +0.6.
Why: Grey palms and a shut door. That's how moonrot gets a start on a
town.

Your note from last turn: Keep it short and practical: get the fever
answer, then say plainly I'll be at the Dunmore door before the
whistle. No lecturing about the mother.

Unresolved, and it matters to you; one comes up when it fits, as a
remark:
- The Dunmore girl with grey spots on her palms, and a mother who
  won't let anyone look at her. I mean to be at that door before the
  mill whistle. [girl: Dunmore, place: the mill]

What you remember:
- The Old well is to be opened. Nobody's drawn from it in twenty years
  and it was capped for a reason. I want to know the reason, and what
  Corvin will do if the water runs foul. [person: Corvin, well: the
  Old well]
```

The model replies from that, then calls `end_turn` with its reply, which
answers `Saved.`.

## Set the same model

The host calls its own model for the replies. Memory is written by the
[`[model]`](/configuration/#model) in `config.toml`, so set the host to the
same model. clanka cannot see or check the host's model.

Without a `[model]`, turns are recorded but never turned into memories, and
`sleep` is unavailable.

## Output

stdout carries the MCP protocol. Notes go to stderr.
